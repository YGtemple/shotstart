品牌名 / Brand Name

品牌定位 / Positioning
ShotStart 面向美国英语市场的新手短视频创作者与小商家，提供简洁拍摄装备与 ShotStarter AI 内容助手。
ShotStart builds simple filming gear and the ShotStarter AI content assistant for beginner short-video creators and small shop owners in the US English-speaking market.
品牌口号 / Slogan
Your first shot starts here.
你的第一个镜头，从这里开始。
品牌故事 / Brand Story
我们从一个朴素的观察出发：开始做短视频最难的不是天赋，而是迈出第一个镜头。
新手创作者和小商家往往有真实的产品和真实的想法，却不知道怎么架手机、构图、把点子写成文案。ShotStart 用直白易用的拍摄装备搭配 ShotStarter AI 助手，降低这道门槛：说明清楚、设置简单，决定权在你手里。
我们不承诺一夜涨粉。我们帮你把第一条视频做完、发出去，然后一条一条继续——用七天挑战的节奏。
ShotStart was built around a simple observation: the hardest part of starting short-video creation isn't talent — it's taking the first shot.
New creators and small shop owners often have a real product and a real message, but they don't know how to set up a phone, frame a shot, or turn an idea into a caption. ShotStart pairs straightforward filming gear with the ShotStarter AI assistant to lower that barrier. We keep instructions plain, setups simple, and decisions in your hands.
We don't promise overnight growth. We help you finish and publish that first video, then the next one — seven days at a time.

橙色使用约束：#FF5A2C 珊瑚橙仅用于大字号展示标题（≥24px 粗体）、行动按钮与图形符号；不用于小字号正文——实测橙/暖白对比度 2.89:1，低于大文本 AA 3:1 边际。小字号文字统一使用海军蓝 #0F1B2D 或 muted #6B6259。如未来需让橙色承载正文，必须改用加深派生色并重新通过对比度测试。
Orange usage constraint: #FF5A2C is reserved for large display headings (≥24px bold), action buttons, and graphic symbols only; it is not used for small-size body text — measured contrast on warm white is 2.89:1, below the large-text AA 3:1 margin. Small text stays navy #0F1B2D or muted #6B6259. If orange ever carries body copy in future, a darkened derivative must be adopted and its contrast retested.
派生色附录：muted 暖灰 #6B6259（次要说明/弱化标签）、备注灰 #555555（仅后台备注）已入表；加深橙色派生色（若日后用于正文）待定，须在选定后补登 Hex 并附对比度实测值。Derived-color appendix: muted warm gray #6B6259 (secondary captions/muted labels) and note gray #555555 (operator notes only) are listed above; a darkened orange derivative (if used for body copy later) is pending — record its Hex and measured contrast once selected.

字体规范 / Typography
标题：粗几何无衬线，推荐 Montserrat 或 Poppins（Bold / SemiBold），字距收紧。Headings: bold geometric sans-serif; Montserrat or Poppins (Bold/SemiBold), tight tracking.
正文：易读常规无衬线，如 Inter 或系统无衬线常规字重。Body: clean readable sans-serif, e.g., Inter or system sans regular weight.
标题回落栈 / Heading stack: Montserrat, Poppins, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif
正文回落栈 / Body stack: Inter, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Arial, sans-serif
避免装饰性或手写字体。Avoid decorative or script typefaces.
语气规范 / Tone of Voice
有活力：语气积极，推动创作者迈出下一步。Energetic: motivated, ready to help creators take the next step.
直白：短句、平实语言，不堆术语和空话。Direct: short sentences, plain language, no jargon or filler.
鼓励新手行动：聚焦把第一条视频做完发出去，站在新手角度说话。Encouraging beginner action: finish and publish that first shot; meet new creators where they are.
诚实：不使用夸大、保证增长或施压话术；AI 草稿仅作起点，需本人审核。Stay honest: no hype, guaranteed-growth claims, or pressure tactics; AI drafts are starting points to review.

海报与图面使用规范 / Poster Usage
白字压图：白色文字叠加实拍/场景图时，须加半透明深色遮罩（或预先加深图面），保证文字对比度可读；遮罩不可用时，优先回退到纯色卡片排版，不要直接把白字压在亮图上。White text over a photo or scene image must sit on a semi-transparent dark overlay (or a pre-darkened image) for readable contrast; when an overlay is not feasible, fall back to a solid-color card layout instead of placing white text directly on a bright image.
海报主标题使用 7-Day Shooting Challenge；按钮 CTA 使用 Join the challenge（见 CTA 矩阵）。Poster headline uses 7-Day Shooting Challenge; the button CTA uses Join the challenge (see the Button Matrix).
