# 7-Day Shooting Challenge

[PUBLISH] Seven short daily tasks. One small step each day to plan, film, and publish your first short video. Sign up with your email to receive daily prompts and ShotStarter template suggestions.

## Rules

- [PUBLISH] Sign up with a valid email; one short challenge email arrives each day for seven days.
- [PUBLISH] Complete one task per day. You may adapt tasks to your product and style.
- [PUBLISH] Use ShotStarter drafts as starting points — review and edit before posting.
- [PUBLISH] The challenge is free to join; no purchase is required.
- [PUBLISH] Participation does not guarantee followers, sales, or any platform result.

## Daily Tasks

[PUBLISH] Day 1 — Pick your topic and selling point. Write down your product or topic and the one point you want viewers to remember. Use ShotStarter to set your platform and product type.

[PUBLISH] Day 2 — Plan your 15-second script. Generate a draft script with ShotStarter; edit the opening line so it sounds like you.

[PUBLISH] Day 3 — Set up your shot. Choose a flat surface or tripod/stand position; clean your lens; frame your first shot.

[PUBLISH] Day 4 — Shoot four storyboard clips. Follow the four-shot storyboard: opening hook, product/topic close-up, middle demonstration, closing line. Record each clip.

[PUBLISH] Day 5 — Assemble your cut. Trim clips to a 15-second pace; add simple captions; check lighting and focus.

[PUBLISH] Day 6 — Write the publish caption. Use the ShotStarter caption draft; add your own voice, relevant hashtags, and a clear call to action.

[PUBLISH] Day 7 — Publish and review. Post your first short video, note what worked, and plan your next clip.
