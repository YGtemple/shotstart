Helpful answers

A few things to know.

Quick answers about the kit and this demonstration storefront.

Who is the Creator Kit for?

It is designed as a simple starter setup for people filming phone tutorials, desk demonstrations, video calls, and everyday creator content.

What exactly comes in the full kit?

The demo bundle contains the adjustable phone tripod, desktop phone stand, and lens cleaning essentials. The phone, laptop, plants, and other scene props are not included.
